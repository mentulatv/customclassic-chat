CustomClassic Chat

A cleaner chat for MapleStory Classic World: megaphones in their own tab, spacer spam gone. It reads the game's chat from a shared window, right in your browser.

  1. Open the chat window and click Share game window. Pick MapleStory's window.
  2. It finds the game's chat by itself, and follows it when you resize it. (Not found? Drag a box around the chat lines.)
  3. Keep the game's chat at least 4 lines tall. More lines miss less in busy places.

Settings

Nothing leaves your computer: the game's picture is read in this browser and never sent anywhere. Third-party, not made or endorsed by Nexon. Want the overlay that sits on the game's chat? Get the Windows app.
