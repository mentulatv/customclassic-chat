CustomClassic Chat

A chat filter for MapleStory Classic World. Megaphones go to their own tab, spacer spam (@@@@@@) is cleaned out, lines read twice are dropped, and your watch list chimes when someone sells what you're after. Guild chat ↔ Discord and Twitch chat sit alongside.

Download for Windows

The Windows app sits right on the game's chat box. All versions and what changed.

  1. Open the chat window and click Share game window. Pick MapleStory's window.
  2. It finds the game's chat by itself, and follows it when you resize it. (Not found? Drag a box around the chat lines.)
  3. Keep the game's chat at least 4 lines tall. More lines miss less in busy places.
